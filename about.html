The Person Behind thefreq.in

Kushal Jaju

Kushal Jaju is a mixing and mastering engineer based in Satara, India, and the person behind every article on thefreq.in. He trained in piano, orchestral writing, and jazz, and cleared his auditions at the Abbey Road Institute before building out his own studio — custom hardware, custom compressors, custom EQs, and a proprietary DAW built specifically around one problem: making a mix hit the same whether someone hears it on AirPods, a car stereo, or a mastering-grade monitor.

His production and mixing credits range from jazz and pop records to large-scale orchestral work, including a 200-plus-track orchestral-to-Latin-jazz recreation of the Game of Thrones theme, and placements such as Atlanta Jazz Radio Station. Clients and collaborators tend to point to the same thing when they work with him: he listens first, and lets that shape the mix, rather than starting from a preset or a plugin chain.

FREQ grew out of that same instinct. Every article on this site — from the psychoacoustics of masking to the newer Arrange for the Ear series on arrangement and production — is an attempt to explain mixing decisions the way he actually reasons through them: starting from how the ear perceives sound, not from a rulebook.
