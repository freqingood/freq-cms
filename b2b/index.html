04 — Sonic QA / QC

Read every mix before anyone says yes.

A mix can be technically clean and still not sound like your label. That gap is invisible until it's on the release — and by then it's a conversation, not a fix.

FREQ reports every incoming mix against your profile, characteristic by characteristic: what sits inside your range, what sits outside it, and by how much.

No score out of 100. A score tells an engineer nothing and tells you less. You get the specific deviation, in language a producer can act on.
