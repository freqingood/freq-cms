01 · The problem

Your sound lives in someone's ears.Nobody can say what it is.

Ten producers. Five mixing engineers. Three studios. Every record passes. Together, they don't sound like one label.

A record comes back from a freelance engineer. It's good. It just isn't you.

“Make it sound more like us” goes back in the notes. Nobody on the thread can define us.

Your sound is the most valuable thing your label owns — and the only asset you have never written down. It needs a fingerprint.
