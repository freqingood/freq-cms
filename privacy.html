Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Applies to thefreq.in and app.thefreq.in

FREQ is a browser-based mixing, mastering and source-separation service run by Alias Analogue Music Studio, Satara, Maharashtra, India. This page says what we collect, where it goes, who else touches it, and how to get it removed. It is written to be read, not to be scrolled past.

Questions, corrections and deletion requests go to freqingoodmix@gmail.com.

2. Your audio

Audio you upload is yours. We claim no rights in it beyond what is needed to store it, process it and play it back to you and the people you share it with.

  • Files go from your browser straight to Google Cloud Storage. Our servers convert them to the project's sample rate when needed, render your mix, and stream it back to you.
  • Source separation, loudness analysis, instrument detection and the mixing engine all run on our own servers and on Google Cloud. Your audio is not sent to any AI company.
  • We listen to your audio only when you ask us to (an appointment, a support request) or when it is strictly necessary to fix a fault you reported.
  • Showcase mixes on thefreq.in are published only with the artist's agreement.

3. Google sign-in and Google Drive

Sign-in. If you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name, profile picture and a Google account id. We use them for your FREQ account only.

Drive import. The app can import stems from your Google Drive. When you use it:

  • You pick files in Google's own file picker. We ask Google for the narrowest permission that exists for this, drive.file, which lets FREQ read only the files you picked. We cannot list, read or change anything else in your Drive.
  • Google gives your browser a short-lived access token. Your browser sends it to our server once per picked file; the server uses it to copy that file into your project's storage and then forgets it. The token is never written to a database or a log.
  • The copy becomes a normal track in your project, covered by section 2. Deleting the track deletes the copy; your original in Drive is never modified.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Imports already made stay in your project until you delete them.

FREQ's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain words: data from your Drive is used only to put the stems you picked into your project. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by people except as described in section 2.

5. When others can see your work

  • Teams. A project moved into a team is visible to every member of that team.
  • Review rooms. People you invite by email can hear the mixes you send to the room, leave comments, and, if you make them approvers, approve a mix. They see the mix, the artist credits you chose to publish, and other reviewers' comments. They cannot reach your tracks, stems or settings.
  • Public mix links. A public link plays that one mix for anyone who has the link, with no sign-in. You can revoke the link at any time and it stops working immediately.
  • Experts and administrators. FREQ staff can open projects to provide support or an expert session. Access is logged.
  • Legal. We disclose data if the law requires it or to protect the service and its users from abuse.

6. How long we keep things

  • Projects, tracks, mixes and settings stay until you delete them. Deleting a track or a project deletes its audio from storage.
  • Upload staging files are deleted as soon as the upload is processed. An upload that never finishes expires after seven days.
  • Links to stored audio that the app hands your browser expire within hours; a review room link expires within six hours and is issued again when you reopen the room.
  • Review invitations that are never accepted expire after fourteen days.
  • Server logs and error reports are kept for a short period for debugging and then discarded.
  • When your account is deleted, your projects, their audio and your review memberships are deleted with it. Comments you left in other people's review rooms are kept with their projects, shown without your name.

7. Cookies and local storage

The app keeps your sign-in session and a few preferences (theme, the view you had open, which workspace you selected) in your browser's local storage. It also caches audio you uploaded in your browser's IndexedDB so waveforms draw without re-downloading; that cache never leaves your device and clearing site data removes it. Firebase Analytics sets its own identifiers to count sessions and feature use. We run no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

8. Security

Every connection uses TLS. Access to projects is checked on the server for every request against who owns the project, who is on its team and who was invited to review it. Audio is served through time-limited signed links, never from a public bucket. Upload sessions are credentials for a single object and are never echoed back in responses. No system is perfect; if you believe something is wrong, write to the address below and we will treat it as urgent.

9. Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. If you are in India, these rights follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, they follow the GDPR, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority. Email freqingoodmix@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will act within thirty days. Account deletion is handled the same way until a self-service button exists in the app.

You can withdraw the Google Drive and Google sign-in permissions from your Google account at any time, as described in section 3.

10. Children

FREQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep an account for anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created one, tell us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

When this page changes, the effective date at the top changes with it. If a change affects what we collect or who we share it with, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
