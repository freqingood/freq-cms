## What Depth-Based Mixing Actually Does

Most digital mixing organises sound on a left-right stereo plane. FREQ adds a front-to-back axis.

Every element in your mix is assigned a depth position. The foreground holds your most present elements; lead vocals, kick, snare. The midground carries harmonic elements. The background holds room tone and texture.

The result is a mix with perceived dimension; not just frequency separation. Elements feel placed, not just balanced.

This depth foundation is what the artist builds and the engineer refines. It is a shared creative language both parties can see, adjust, and agree on.
