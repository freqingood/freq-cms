Business & Client Workflow

Pricing, communication, and the working relationship between a mixing engineer and their clients.

  • How Do You Calculate Your Fee for One Song?

    A practical way to calculate a sustainable mixing fee for one song by accounting for time, studio costs, overhead, revisions, business expenses, and the value of your experience.

  • How to Honour Everyone's Vision When Mixing a Band or Team

    How mixers can balance different opinions in a band or creative team while protecting the song, respecting each person's contribution, and keeping the project moving.

  • Should You Charge Hourly or Per Project for Mixing?

    Hourly or project-based mixing rates? Understand the trade-offs between hourly and fixed project pricing for home studios, rented studios, and professional mixing businesses.

  • Your Mix, Your Rules ; Why the Artist-Engineer Handoff Is Broken (And How to Fix It)

    A practical guide to deciding when to mix a song yourself and when bringing in an experienced mixing engineer makes more sense.

  • How to Manage a Mixing Project Without Losing the Creative Process

    Good project management can protect the creative process instead of restricting it. Learn a practical way to organize mixing projects, communication, revisions, files, and delivery.

  • Producer's Notes to Prevent Communication Loss

    A practical guide to giving mix revision notes that actually work: describe what you hear instead of prescribing the fix, use specific references, separate problems from preferences, and prioritize notes so revisions stay efficient.

  • How to Request Revisions Without the Endless-Revision Trap

    Learn how to give mixing revision feedback that actually improves a song: listen to the full mix first, separate real problems from preferences, consolidate notes, and recognize when a mix is finished.

  • How to Talk to a Mixing Client

    Learn how to communicate with mixing clients with patience, honesty, reassurance, and clear expectations. Good client communication is part of being a professional mixer.
