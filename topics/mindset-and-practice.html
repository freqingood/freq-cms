Mindset & Practice

The working life of a mixing engineer: patience, curiosity, and staying sharp between sessions.

  • Why Feel Matters More Than Technical Perfection in a Mix

    A technically perfect mix can still feel wrong. Learn why musical feel, phrasing, dynamics and emotional impact should often take priority over perfectly controlled vocals, bass and timing.

  • How to Keep the Joy of Discovery While Mixing

    Mixing can become a constant search for problems. Learn how curiosity, exploration, and fresh listening can keep the creative process enjoyable and improve your decisions.

  • Why Songs From Before Modern Mixing Tools Still Sound Great

    What can modern mixing engineers learn from records made before today's editing and processing tools? Discover why older recordings remain valuable references for balance, performance, arrangement and feel.

  • Should You Work in Teams as a Mixing Engineer?

    Should mixing engineers work alone or in teams? Explore the advantages of collaboration, when specialists help, and how to build a team without losing your creative identity.

  • What Should You Do on Days When You Have No Mixing Work?

    Slow days are an opportunity to train as a mixer. Learn how to use time without client work to experiment with plugins, techniques, workflows, and new approaches.
