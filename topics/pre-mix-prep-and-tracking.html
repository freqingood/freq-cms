Pre-Mix Prep & Tracking

What to sort out before a mix session even begins: organizing tracks, editing pitch and timing, and layering live and programmed instruments.

  • Why Layering Live and Programmed Instruments Creates Better Texture

    Learn why layering a live performance with a programmed version of the same instrument can create depth, texture and consistency without simply adding more tracks.

  • Why MIDI-to-Tone Matters Before Mixing

    Why the right MIDI, samples and live performances matter before mixing. Learn how source quality, realistic instrument ranges, articulation and performance choices create better tones and more space in a mix.

  • How to Organise a Mixing Session Before You Mix (The Step Most People Skip)

    A messy session ruins a mix before it starts. Learn how to upload, name, detect, and group your multitrack files so your mix begins from a clean, organised foundation in FREQ.

  • Why Pitch and Timing Edits Should Usually Happen Before Mixing

    Pitch and timing corrections can fundamentally change a performance. Learn why these decisions are usually better made during production, before the mix engineer starts shaping the final sound.

  • How to Prepare Your Multitrack Files Like a Pro Before You Mix

    A great mix is won before you upload. Learn the professional pre-flight checklist for preparing multitrack files: matched length, sample rate, bit depth, headroom, and clean labelling for FREQ.

  • Why One Live Instrument Can Change Everything in a Mix

    A single live performance can change the feel, depth and balance of an entire mix. Learn why real dynamics, timing, texture and interaction can matter more than adding another layer or plugin.
